
    An AI Engineer designs, builds, and deploys applications powered by artificial intelligence.
They use programming languages like Python and work with machine learning and deep learning models.
AI Engineers integrate technologies such as LLMs, RAG, vector databases, and APIs into real-world applications.
They preprocess data, train or fine-tune models, and optimize AI systems for accuracy and performance.
They collaborate with software developers and data scientists to create scalable AI solutions.
AI Engineers continuously test, monitor, and improve AI applications to ensure reliability, efficiency, and a good user experience.
